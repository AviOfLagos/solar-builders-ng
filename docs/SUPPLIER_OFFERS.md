# Getting brands to talk to us and give good offers

**Core idea:** brands answer volume and proof, not requests for discounts. Show them demand first, then ask for a distributor price.

## What to bring to every brand
- A one-page deck: Lagos market, what we sell, orders per month `[TBC real numbers]`, how we deliver and install.
- Real traffic and lead numbers from /admin (visitors, leads, quotes). Ask only after you have them.
- A clear offer: "We drive Lagos demand to your products. We want distributor pricing and stock priority."

## Who to contact, in order
1. **Felicity, itel, Sun King, Arnergy, EcoFlow**: Nigerian sales/distribution managers on LinkedIn, plus the "become a dealer/distributor" form on each brand site.
2. Their **existing Lagos distributors** (Alaba, Ikeja, Computer Village solar traders): ask for tier pricing at 5/10/20 units. Faster than the brand.
3. Ask each for: dealer price list, MOQ, warranty and RMA process, stock lead time, co-marketing support.

## What makes them say yes
- A **feature page** for their brand on our site and in Go Solar Me kits (issue #4 brand wall).
- **Order forecasts**: "we'll move ~N units/month" backed by lead data.
- **Fast payment** and clean installer reports (they care about warranty claims done right).
- Exclusive "official partner" badge in return for better pricing.

## 14-day plan
- Day 1-2: deck + price comparison sheet. Day 3-5: send 5 brand emails + 5 distributor WhatsApps. Day 6-10: follow up, take calls. Day 11-14: compare offers in /admin/suppliers, pick one per product line, set cost per product (issue #8).

## Rule
Do not take orders until supplier stock is confirmed in writing; keep the payment switch OFF until then.
